## Pseudo Paragraph

Recent reviews suggest that structured retrieval pipelines improve literature coverage while keeping screening decisions auditable (Lee & Carter, 2021; Nguyen et al., 2023). The same studies argue that transparent logging is especially important when iterative citation expansion is used because each expansion round can introduce topic drift if screening criteria are not restated clearly (Lee & Carter, 2021).
