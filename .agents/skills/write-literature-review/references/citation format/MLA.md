# MLA Example

## Pseudo Paragraph

Recent scholarship argues that structured retrieval pipelines can improve literature coverage while preserving an auditable screening trail (Lee and Carter 108; Nguyen et al. 49). The same authors note that citation expansion is most reliable when each screening round repeats the review scope and exclusion logic in writing (Lee and Carter 112).

## Works Cited

Lee, Maya, and Jordan Carter. "Auditable Workflows for Systematic Literature Mapping." *Journal of Research Synthesis*, vol. 14, no. 2, 2021, pp. 101-119.

Nguyen, Priya, et al. "Iterative Citation Expansion for High-Recall Evidence Reviews." *International Journal of Digital Scholarship*, vol. 9, no. 1, 2023, pp. 44-63.
